## User Pain Points

### Pre-Interview Phase

Pain Point | Current Solution | Our Solution
--- | --- | ---
Uncertainty about interview expectations | Generic FAQs | Role-specific configuration with defined difficulty levels
Limited time available for practice | Expensive coaching or limited access | On-demand practice sessions available at any time
Lack of clarity on answer quality | No structured feedback | Real-time diagnostic evaluation
Repetition of the same mistakes | Manual tracking | Aggregated session reports highlighting recurring patterns

---

### During Practice

Pain Point | Current Solution | Our Solution
--- | --- | ---
Questions do not align with target role | Generic question banks | Job description-driven question generation
Lack of understanding of performance gaps | Limited or no explanation | Detailed diagnostic feedback with actionable examples
Inconsistent interviewer behaviour | Variable human feedback | Configurable personas ensuring consistent tone
Limited guidance on next steps | Manual search for resources | Context-aware follow-up questions

---

### Post-Interview Phase

Pain Point | Current Solution | Our Solution
--- | --- | ---
Unclear improvement path | Vague coaching notes | Structured improvement plan with defined focus areas
Difficulty identifying strengths | Self-assessment | Data-driven analysis of strengths and weaknesses
Limited ability to track progress | Manual note-taking | Session-level reporting and trend analysis
Difficulty in explaining reasoning | No structured evaluation of thinking | Structured evaluation of decision-making, trade-offs, and reasoning quality
