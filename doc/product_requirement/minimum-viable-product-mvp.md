## Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

### Scope

The MVP is the smallest complete interview practice experience. It covers the base interview loop and the basic report download required to complete a session end to end.

### MVP Success Criteria

1. Users can complete a full interview practice session from welcome screen to session summary.
2. Users can submit answers, receive validation, and progress through the interview loop.
3. AI evaluation is consistent across interviewer personas.
4. Feedback is specific, actionable, and non-generic.
5. Basic report downloads are available at the end of the session.
6. Validation and security controls prevent misuse.
7. API-driven interactions remain within acceptable performance thresholds.
8. The UI remains clear, stable, and distraction-free.
